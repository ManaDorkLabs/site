Proof of concept

The Agora

The first cooperative marketplace built on our infrastructure — and the argument that the rest of it works.

What it has to prove

A proof of concept is not a demo. The Agora only counts if it carries real sellers, real buyers and real money, and comes out the other side with economics that hold.

  • That it functions under load. A live marketplace, not a prototype, handling real volume.
  • That the fee model works. Ultra-low fees that still cover operation, with no third party taking rent in between.
  • That democratic governance survives contact with users. Members voting on the rules of the market they trade in, at a coordination cost the business can actually absorb.
  • That the core is portable. Whatever is not specific to this one market gets carved out and reused by the cooperatives that follow.

Status

Not yet announced The name is a working title and the market has not been chosen. We are evaluating candidates against one question: which market lets a cooperative bootstrap fastest without compromising on the governance? This page fills in once that is settled.

If you run, build for, or sell into a market you think fits that description, we would genuinely like to hear about it.
