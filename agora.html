The Agora

The proof of concept

We are developing the Agora as a proof of concept. It will demonstrate the viability and scalability of a cooperative marketplace.

What it will demonstrate

  • That a cooperative marketplace is viable, and that it scales
  • That it can function and handle real load
  • That the economic models hold, and earn real revenue
  • A set of core features that can be copied into additional marketplace cooperative iterations in other domains

Status

Not yet announced The name is a work in progress, and we have not settled on the marketplace yet. We are looking for the one that best fits our bootstrapping needs. This page fills in once that is decided.

If you know a market that fits, we want to hear about it.
