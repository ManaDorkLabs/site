## The proof of concept

We are developing the Agora as a proof of concept. It will demonstrate the
viability and scalability of a cooperative marketplace.

> The goal: show that an alternative exists, prove that it can work, then SCALE.

From the proof of concept, MDL will carve out a set of core features that can
be copied into additional marketplace cooperative iterations in other domains.
The capital raised from the first successfully scaled marketplace will be used
to branch into more marketplaces and establish more cooperatives.

## Key elements of the marketplace infrastructure

- Cooperative ownership to avoid extractive marketplace rents from a 3rd party
- Simple, intuitive user interfaces with minimal set up friction
- Blockchain technology to access next generation cost reductions in payment rail operation and governance execution
