## Proof of Concept

We are developing the **Agora** as a proof of concept. It will demonstrate the viability and scalability of a cooperative marketplace. Our goal: show that an alternative to 3rd party marketplaces exists, prove it can work, then **SCALE**.

Upon the successful proof of concept, MDL will carve out the set of core features applicable to all cooperative marketplaces. With the capital raised from the cash flow of the first successfully scaled marketplace, MDL will pursue the creation and growth of more cooperative marketplaces.

## Key tenets of our marketplace infrastructure

- Minimize friction
- Minimize fees
- Maximize user ownership
