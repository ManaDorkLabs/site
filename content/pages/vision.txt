## The State of Play

Traditional online marketplaces are 3rd party endeavors run by neither the buyers nor the sellers. They charge exorbitant fees, offer no say in how the marketplace is run, and exist purely as a middleman working to skim maximum profit of the top of every transaction. Mana Dork Labs exists to change that.

## The Missing Piece

An agora was a central public space in ancient Greek city-states, and represented the center of the athletic, artistic, business, social, spiritual, and political life of the city.

In honor of that public commons, Mana Dork Labs is developing the **Agora**, our cooperative marketplace infrastructure. The goal of the Agora is to show that an alternative to 3rd party marketplaces can be built, prove that it can work, then demonstrate that it can **scale**.

Once the first successful iteration is demonstrated, Mana Dork Labs will broaden the Agora, expanding it to work for more marketplaces, which will in turn enable more cooperatives to flourish.
