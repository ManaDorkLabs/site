## Proof of Concept

We are developing the Agora as a proof of concept. It will demonstrate the
viability and scalability of a cooperative marketplace.

Our goal: show that an alternative to extraction exists, prove that it can work, then SCALE.

From a successful proof of concept, MDL will carve out a set of core features which can
be re-used for additional cooperative marketplaces in other domains.
The capital raised from the cash flow of the first successfully scaled marketplace will facilitate
the creation and growth of more cooperative marketplaces.

## Key tenets of our marketplace infrastructure

- Minimize friction
- Minimize fees
- Maximize cooperative ownership
