## The State of Play

Traditional online marketplaces are 3rd party endeavors owned and run by neither the buyers nor the sellers. They charge exorbitant fees, offer no say in how the marketplace is run, and exist purely as a middleman working to skim maximum profit of the top of every transaction. Cooperativism in online marketplaces solves that problem, but capital formation for cooperatives is notoriously difficult, which in turn makes it near impossible for early cooperatives to build the infrastructure for both their marketplace and their governance. **Mana Dork Labs exists to change that.**

## The Missing Piece

An agora was a central public space in ancient Greek city-states, and represented the center of the city's athletic, artistic, business, social, spiritual, and political life.

Mana Dork Labs is developing the [Agora](/agora), our cooperative marketplace infrastructure, named in honor of that public commons. The goal of the Agora is to provide early platform cooperatives with the capability to spin up their infrastructure at the click of a button and with minimal upfront cost.

Over time, Mana Dork Labs plans to broaden the Agora, expanding it to work for more marketplaces, and eventually even non-marketplace cooperatives via the governance elements. As usage of the Agora grows, the costs to operate each individual instance of it will decline due to economies of scale, which will in turn further enable even more cooperatives to flourish.
