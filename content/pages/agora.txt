## Key Principles of the Agora

- Minimize friction
- Minimize fees
- Maximize user ownership

## The Agora's Demonstration Goals

- Show that a cooperative marketplace is viable and scales
- Prove that a DLT-based system can function and handle real load
- Validate the economic models and earn real revenue
- Identify the set of core features that every marketplace needs, while maintaining modularity for usage in other markets
