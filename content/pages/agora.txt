## Key Principles of the Agora

- Minimize friction for transactions and governance
- Minimize the costs of operation
- Maximize the cooperative's ownership of the marketplace

## The Agora's Demonstration Goals

- Show that a cooperative marketplace is viable and scales
- Prove that the Agora's DLT-based systems can function and handle real load
- Validate the economic models and earn real revenue
- Stress-test the set of core features that every cooperative marketplace needs, while maintaining modularity for usage across multiple markets
