## Core Values: Ikigai

We derive our core values from the Japanese concept of ikigai (translated literally: "a reason for being").

:::values
- Do something you love
- Do something you are good at 
- Do something you can get paid for 
- Do something that the world needs
:::

> Mana Dork Labs is our ikigai.

### Rob

Aerospace engineer with a history of developing MVPs in a fast-paced environment under extreme constraints.

---

Motivation:

I have spent my career building spacecraft under brutal constraints. In so doing, I learned that the hardest part of engineering is almost never developing the technology itself; instead, I've found it to be the **alignment of people**. When I realized the potential of platform cooperatives and how underserved they are for their infrastructure, I became determined to build Mana Dork Labs so that we could solve the alignment problem at scale for online marketplaces.
