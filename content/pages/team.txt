## Core Values: Ikigai

We derive our core values from the Japanese concept of ikigai (translated literally: "a reason for being").

:::values
- Do something you love
- Do something you are good at 
- Do something you can get paid for 
- Do something that the world needs
:::

> Mana Dork Labs is our ikigai.

### Rob

Aerospace engineer with experience developing MVPs in a fast-paced environment under extreme constraints.

Rob's Motivation:
I have spent my career building spacecraft under brutal constraints. In so doing, I learned that the hardest part of most systems is almost never developing the actual technical solutions; instead, it lies in aligning everyone on the project towards the same goal. When I realized the potential of platform cooperatives and how underserved they are for infrastructure, I became determined to build Mana Dork Labs and deliver the solution to the alignment problem for online marketplaces.
