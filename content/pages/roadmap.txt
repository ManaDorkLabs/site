## Stages

:::stages
- [now] Develop the core technology
- Bootstrap with an existing or growing cooperative marketplace
- Go live and prove that the designs can handle real load
- Validate the economic models and generate viable cash flows
- Expand into other cooperative marketplaces, bolstered by capital flows from the successful proof of concept
- Democratize capital, enable more cooperatives, and save the world
:::
