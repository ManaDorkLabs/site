## Stages

:::stages
- [now] Develop the core infrastructure
- Bootstrap with an existing or new cooperative marketplace
- Go live and prove that the systems can handle real load
- Validate the economic models and generate meaningful cash flows
- Expand into other cooperative marketplaces, bolstered by capital flows from the successful proof of concept
- Democratize capital, enable even more cooperatives beyond just marketplaces, and save the world
:::
