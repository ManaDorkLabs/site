## Stages

:::stages
- [now] Develop the core infrastructure for [the Agora](/agora)
- Bootstrap with an existing or currently forming cooperative marketplace
- Go live and prove that the Agora can handle real load
- Validate MDL's economic models and generate meaningful cash flow
- Expand into other cooperative marketplaces, bolstered by revenue from the successful proof of concept
- Democratize capital, enable even more cooperatives beyond just marketplaces, and save the world
:::
