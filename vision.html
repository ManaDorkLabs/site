The Vision

What we’re building

Mana Dork Labs builds cooperative online marketplaces with democratic governance and ultra-low fees.

The proof of concept

We are developing the Agora as our proof of concept. It exists to demonstrate two things: that a cooperative marketplace is viable, and that it scales.

Show that an alternative exists, prove that it can work, then scale.

From that first marketplace we carve out a set of core features — the parts that are not specific to any one domain — and copy them into further marketplace cooperatives elsewhere. Capital raised from the first marketplace to scale successfully funds the next ones, and the cooperatives they establish.

The infrastructure

Three things have to be true of the platform underneath all of it.

  • Cooperative ownership. No third party sitting in the middle extracting marketplace rents from the people doing the work.
  • Interfaces that get out of the way. Simple, intuitive, with minimal set-up friction. A marketplace nobody can be bothered to join proves nothing.
  • Blockchain where it earns its place. Next-generation cost reductions in payment rail operation and governance execution — not as a badge, as a line item.
