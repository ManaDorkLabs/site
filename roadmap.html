The Roadmap

Where we are

Core technology, then a proof of concept, then more cooperatives.

Stages

  1. Develop the core technologyCurrent
  2. Find the proof of concept marketplace that best fits our bootstrapping needs
  3. Go live with the marketplace and prove that it can function / handle real load
  4. Validate the economic models and earn real revenue
  5. Expand into other cooperative marketplaces, bolstered by capital flows from the successful proof of concept marketplace
  6. Save the world!
