The Roadmap

Where we are

Six stages. Each one has to actually hold before the next one is worth starting.

Stages

  1. Develop the core technologyCurrent

    The marketplace primitives every later cooperative inherits — payments, governance, escrow, identity.

  2. Choose the proof-of-concept marketplace

    Find the market that best fits our bootstrapping needs: small enough to reach, real enough to prove the economics.

  3. Go live

    Launch the marketplace and prove it can function and handle real load, with real people and real money moving through it.

  4. Validate the economics

    Confirm the models hold at operating scale and earn real revenue.

  5. Expand

    Branch into further cooperative marketplaces, bolstered by capital flows from the successful proof of concept.

  6. Save the world

    The part we are least likely to finish on schedule.
